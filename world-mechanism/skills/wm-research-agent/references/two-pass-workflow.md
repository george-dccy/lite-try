# Two-pass Research Workflow

## Why two passes

Research before creative work and research after creative work solve different problems.

### Pass A asks:
> Are we building on reality correctly?

It should be:
- fast;
- selective;
- mechanism-oriented;
- willing to kill or redefine the premise.

### Pass B asks:
> Can every important factual part of this chosen Blueprint survive scrutiny?

It should be:
- systematic;
- auditable;
- production-aware;
- precise about scope and wording.

---

## Pass A anti-patterns
- collecting twenty sources before defining the question;
- searching only for support;
- turning research into a literature review;
- writing the article while researching.

## Pass B anti-patterns
- letting research rewrite the approved creative premise without surfacing the conflict;
- attaching one citation to a paragraph with five different claims;
- using generic sources when a primary source is available;
- omitting evidence that weakens the preferred framing.

---

## Hand-off from Pass A to Mechanism Explorer

Pass A returns:
- premise verdict;
- operational definitions;
- key facts;
- alternative explanation;
- research gaps.

Mechanism Explorer decides what model changes.

## Hand-off from Blueprint to Pass B

Blueprint provides:
- exact mechanism;
- Must Preserve;
- candidate factual claims;
- expression mode.

Pass B returns:
- Evidence Pack;
- claim wording limits;
- visual / archival candidates;
- unresolved caveats.

The content writer receives the Blueprint and Evidence Pack separately.
