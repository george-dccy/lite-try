# Counterfactual Protocol v0.2

Counterfactuals are diagnostic instruments, not decoration.

## Step 0 — Operationalize the rule

Before predicting consequences, state:

- **Removed:** what exact capability / rule disappears?
- **Preserved:** what still works?
- **Allowed substitutes:** what mechanisms can replace it?
- **Scope:** which people / places / systems are affected?
- **Duration:** temporary experiment or permanent world rule?

If these are unclear, stop. The counterfactual is not ready.

## Operations

### Remove
Delete an ability, signal, institution, resource, or shortcut.

### Expose
Make an invisible cost, rule, dependency, or signal visible.

### Amplify
Increase a weak effect until adaptation becomes obvious.

### Delay / Accelerate
Change the time between action and consequence.

### Localize
Make a rule apply only to one place, group, relationship, or layer.

### Invert
Swap who owns information, pays cost, receives benefit, or makes the decision.

## Consequence ladder

Use when the system is deterministic enough:

1. **First order** — What changes immediately?
2. **Second order** — How do others adapt?
3. **Third order** — What institution, relationship, market, norm, or technology changes after adaptation becomes normal?

## Branching scenarios

Use when multiple substitutions are plausible or social behavior is highly uncertain.

For each branch:
- replacement mechanism;
- likely beneficiaries;
- likely cost-bearers;
- new bottleneck;
- new feedback loop;
- what evidence would be needed to know whether this branch dominates.

Never merge multiple branches into one “and then society will…” story unless the work is explicitly fictional.

## Strength test

A strong counterfactual:
- creates several behaviors without adding new magic;
- reveals trade-offs;
- exposes where power / cost moves;
- affects both beneficiaries and cost-bearers;
- distinguishes facts from scenario hypotheses.

A weak counterfactual:
- generates one visual gag;
- requires new exceptions every scene;
- only scales by affecting more people;
- exists only to deliver a predetermined moral;
- writes a speculative social timeline as certainty.
