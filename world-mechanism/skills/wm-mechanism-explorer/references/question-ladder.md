# Question Ladder

Use selectively. The purpose is not to fill every section but to find the next question that changes the model.

## Phenomenon
- What exactly is happening?
- What is the smallest surprising detail?
- Who experiences it differently?
- When does it not happen?

## Causality
- What is a direct cause?
- What is merely correlated?
- Which condition is necessary but insufficient?
- What would falsify this explanation?

## Incentives
- What is each actor optimizing?
- Who gets the benefit?
- Who bears the cost?
- What behavior is locally rational but systemically harmful?

## System
- What feeds back into itself?
- Where are the bottlenecks?
- Is there a threshold or network effect?
- What keeps the system stable?
- What makes it brittle?

## Information
- Who knows what?
- Who chooses with incomplete information?
- What signal substitutes for missing knowledge?
- What changes if everyone sees the same information?

## Time
- What is the short-term adaptation?
- What only appears after repeated use?
- Which past decision locks in the present?
- Does the mechanism reverse over longer horizons?

## Human behavior
- What is the first everyday action that changes?
- Who adapts fastest?
- What new habit appears?
- Which old habit suddenly becomes costly?
- What does refusing to act now mean?

## Creator
- Which question makes you personally curious?
- Which answer feels technically correct but dead?
- Which detail connects to your own experience?
- What would be worth discussing rather than teaching?
